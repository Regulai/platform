Mira was a brilliant scientist with raven-black hair that framed her sharp features, always lit from within by an unquenchable passion for her work. However, they had both been so absorbed in their own separate projects that important correspondence had slipped through the cracks.

Feeling the weight of the situation, Zane decided to call upon Luna. “Hey, Luna, could you send a quick mail to Mira?”

“Certainly, Zane. What would you like the message to say?” Luna chimed, her tone reassuring and efficient.

A soft vibration emanated from Luna as she processed the request. “Triggering mail AI Agent. Sending now...”

It was typical Zane—wildly brilliant yet utterly endearing in his own, unique way.
