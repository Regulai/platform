About Obfuscation

Obfuscation masks detected personal data and secrets in prompts, context, documents and responses. Choose local processing to keep originals on this server.

How it works:
  • Detects PII (names, emails, phones, etc.)
  • Detects secrets (API keys, tokens, etc.)
  • Replaces detected data with stable placeholders
  • Multiple configs are chained in sequence

Local processing needs the detector model installed on the server. Its incremental API cost is zero: set the policy's masking maximum call cost to 0 for a local-only chain. HTTP configurations require their own contractual call bound. Detection can miss sensitive data; YARA and the company policy still apply.
